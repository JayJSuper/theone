Figure 1. On the de-anchored generator (true value spread off the visible-CPT mean, so no guessing shortcut — §5.3), interventional-query accuracy collapses for every LLM family while the engine is exact at every 2ᵏ. The cliff is universal (all three families reach 0.00 by k=6) but its location slides with capability and token budget: deepseek-v4-flash fails at k=4, gpt-5.1 at k=5, and gemini-2.5-pro — a heavy reasoner given a 6× output budget — holds until k=6. A bigger budget buys exactly one notch, never an escape. Protocol-failure counts as error (AM-007); every engine value is independently recomputed by pgmpy to <10⁻⁶ (AM-016). The claim is therefore *exact-and-cheap vs approximate-and-expensive within an enumerable, known-structure regime* — not *tractable vs intractable* (the engine is itself O(2ᵏ); see §6).
