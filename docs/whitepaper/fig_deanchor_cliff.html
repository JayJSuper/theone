Figure 1. On the de-anchored generator (true value spread off the visible-cell mean, no guessing shortcut), exact interventional accuracy collapses for every LLM family, but the cliff's location is capability-ordered: deepseek-v4-flash at 2⁴=16, gpt-5.1 at 2⁵=32, gemini-2.5-pro at 2⁶=64, and the frontier claude-opus-4.8 holds to 2⁶=64 (0.85) before collapsing at 2⁷=128 (0.33→0.08). The collapse is computational (confident wrong answers, not budget — claude's k=7 errors carry no protocol failures). No model escapes the cliff; the engine has none, at every k, at ~zero token cost, with an independently-recomputable credential. The claim is therefore exact-and-cheap-and-verifiable with no cliff vs a capability-bounded cliff every model eventually hits — not the weaker "LLMs can't." Protocol-failure = error (AM-007); engine values pgmpy-verified <10⁻⁶. Reproducible: experiments/deanchor_cliff/.
