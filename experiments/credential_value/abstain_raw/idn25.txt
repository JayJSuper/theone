1)) = \sum_{u} P(Y=1 \mid X=1,u)\,P(u).
\]

Given:
- \(P(U=1)=0.36 \Rightarrow P(U=0)=0.64\)
- \(P(Y=1 \mid X=1,U=1)=0.72\)
- \(P(Y=1 \mid X=1,U=0)=0.37\)

So
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=1 \mid do(X=1)) &= 0.72 \cdot 0.36 + 0.37 \cdot 0.64 \\
&= 0.2592 + 0.2368 \\
&= 0.496.
\end{aligned}
\]

FINAL: 0.496
