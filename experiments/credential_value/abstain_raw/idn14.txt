

Given:
- \(P(U=1)=0.66\) so \(P(U=0)=0.34\),
- \(P(Y=1\mid X=1,U=1)=0.79\),
- \(P(Y=1\mid X=1,U=0)=0.28\).

Thus:
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=1\mid do(X=1))
&= 0.79\times 0.66 + 0.28\times 0.34 \\
&= 0.5214 + 0.0952 \\
&= 0.6166.
\end{aligned}
\]

So \(P(Y=1\mid do(X=1)) \approx 0.617\).

FINAL: 0.6166
