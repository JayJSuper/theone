} P(Y=1 \mid X=1,u)\,P(u).
\]

Given:
- \(P(U=1) = 0.49 \Rightarrow P(U=0) = 0.51\)
- \(P(Y=1 \mid X=1, U=1) = 0.78\)
- \(P(Y=1 \mid X=1, U=0) = 0.30\)

So:

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=1 \mid do(X=1))
&= 0.78 \cdot 0.49 + 0.30 \cdot 0.51 \\
&= 0.3822 + 0.153 \\
&= 0.5352.
\end{aligned}
\]

FINAL: 0.5352
