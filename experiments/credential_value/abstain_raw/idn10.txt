 X=1,U=1) = 0.48\)
- \(P(Y=1\mid X=1,U=0) = 0.78\)

So:

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=1\mid do(X=1)) 
&= P(Y=1\mid X=1, U=1)P(U=1) + P(Y=1\mid X=1, U=0)P(U=0) \\
&= 0.48 \cdot 0.38 + 0.78 \cdot 0.62 \\
&= 0.1824 + 0.4836 \\
&= 0.666.
\end{aligned}
\]

So \(P(Y=1\mid do(X=1)) \approx 0.666\).

FINAL: 0.666
