(U=1)=0.54 \Rightarrow P(U=0)=0.46\)  
- \(P(Y=1\mid X=1, U=1)=0.32\)  
- \(P(Y=1\mid X=1, U=0)=0.26\)

Compute:

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=1\mid do(X=1)) 
&= 0.32 \cdot 0.54 + 0.26 \cdot 0.46 \\
&= 0.1728 + 0.1196 \\
&= 0.2924.
\end{aligned}
\]

So \(P(Y=1\mid do(X=1)) \approx 0.2924.\)

FINAL: 0.2924
