 X=1,U=1)=0.51\).
- \(P(Y=1\mid X=1,U=0)=0.44\).

So
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=1\mid do(X=1))
&= P(Y=1\mid X=1,U=1)P(U=1) + P(Y=1\mid X=1,U=0)P(U=0)\\

\]
