 0.52\),
- \(P(Y=1 \mid X=1, U=0) = 0.63\).

Thus:
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=1\mid do(X=1)) 
&= P(Y=1\mid X=1, U=1)\,P(U=1) + P(Y=1\mid X=1, U=0)\,P(U=0) \\
&= 0.52 \cdot 0.68 + 0.63 \cdot 0.32 \\
&= 0.3536 + 0.2016 \\
&= 0.5552.
\end{aligned}
\]

So \(P(Y=1\mid do(X=1)) \approx 0.555\).
